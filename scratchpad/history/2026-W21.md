## iOS auth crash — root cause and fix (05-23/24)
TestFlight build 13 produced two user-reported crashes (sign-out, delete
account) on iPhone16,2 / iOS 26.1. Both aborted through `RCTFatal` in
`ExpoNotificationsPermissionsRequester.getPermissions()`.

**Fix:** removed every automatic `expo-notifications` call from auth/user
bootstrap — no `getPermissionsAsync()` or `requestPermissionsAsync()` from
`apps/mobile/src`. Added native auth storage helpers that inspect Better Auth's
persisted cookie/session cache; the index route now briefly refetches and shows
the loader when a persisted native session exists instead of redirecting straight
to `/sign-in`. Sign-out explicitly clears the persisted native cache in the
fallback path.

**Verified** on an iOS simulator runner (iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.3) driving the
real UI with `idb`: sign-out returns to sign-in without crashing, delete-account
confirmation returns to sign-in without crashing, and session persistence
survives terminate/relaunch (app reopens to Projects). Full lint, typecheck, and
test gates passed locally and on the runner.

# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should remain respectful, specific, and useful. Contributors must communicate without harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or disclosure of another person's private information.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Feedback should address the work, explain impact, and leave room for correction.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other participation that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Maintainers will handle reports with appropriate privacy and will avoid conflicts of interest when assigning a reviewer.
